# Materiality report

The C4 experiment compares the best fair nominal-information MPC with evaluation-only current-capability O2 under identical horizon, action budget, causal load estimator and SG A/B/C balance. It uses validation seeds 100–119 only. Percent improvements are scenario-balanced ratios of aggregate sums, with a seed-within-scenario bootstrap; mean episode-wise ratios are not used as the conclusion. See `results_phase_c/C4/materiality_summary.json` for the locked numerical decision.
