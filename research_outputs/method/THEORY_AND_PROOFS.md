# Conditional theory and proofs

**Set coverage.** If measurement error is bounded by the declared inflation and a change alarm resets the lower bound before the next control action, the update cannot exclude the true scalar capability.

**Recursive feasibility.** Assume the true capability remains in the interval, load-estimation error belongs to the declared disturbance set, and the SG-only backup can absorb the residual within reserve/GRC constraints. Shifting the feasible sequence and appending backup yields a feasible successor.

**Constraint safety.** The IBR command is limited by the interval lower bound and SG by reserve. Under the preceding assumptions and successful update, physical constraints hold; on invalid measurements, SG-only backup is used. These are conditional results, not an unconditional guaranteed-safe claim.
